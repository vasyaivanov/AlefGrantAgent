# FAA Aviation Research Grants (NOFO 20-01) — ELIGIBILITY BLOCKER

**Status: BLOCKED for Alef as a direct (prime) applicant. Awaiting CEO decision.**

## The gate
NOFO 20-01 limits eligible applicants to **colleges, universities, and other non-profit 501(c)(3) research institutions** (plus professional associations, hospitals, labs, museums with non-profit status). **Individuals and for-profit organizations are excluded** as prime recipients. A narrow exception exists only for research in **aviation-security technologies**.

Alef Aeronautics is a **for-profit company** → **not eligible to be the prime applicant** under the general program.
